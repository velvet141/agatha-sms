# Privacy Policy

**Brand:** Victoria - Personal ("Agatha alerts")

This policy covers the Agatha alerts SMS program operated by Victoria - Personal. The program sends personal assistant notifications to a single recipient: the account owner.

## What data we collect
- The recipient's mobile phone number.
- The content of messages sent to and from the program number, and their timestamps.
- Opt-in and opt-out records (START, Y, STOP).

## How it is used
This data is used only to deliver the notifications the account owner requested, to respond to HELP and STOP, and to keep a record of consent. It is not used for marketing.

## Sharing
We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Messages are carried by our SMS provider (Twilio) solely to deliver them.

## Retention
Message records are kept only as long as needed to operate the service and are deleted on request.

## Opting out
Reply **STOP** at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply **HELP** for help.

## Contact
Questions about this policy can be sent by replying HELP to the program number.
