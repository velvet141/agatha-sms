# Agatha alerts: how to opt in

**Program:** Victoria - Personal ("Agatha alerts")

## Who sends and who receives
Agatha alerts are personal notifications sent by Victoria's self-hosted assistant software ("Agatha") to one recipient only: Victoria, the account owner, on her own mobile phone. There is no public sign-up, contact list, marketing or third-party messaging.

## How consent is given
1. From her own mobile phone, the account owner texts **START** to the program number.
2. The program replies with a confirmation:
   > Victoria - Personal (Agatha alerts): Reply Y to confirm personal alerts from your assistant. Up to 30 msgs/day. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.
3. She replies **Y** to finish opting in. Nothing else is sent until she does.

## What messages look like
- Task finished: "Victoria - Personal (Agatha alerts): Your backup finished. Reply STOP to opt out."
- Power alert: "Victoria - Personal (Agatha alerts): Power outage at home, running on battery."
- Reminder: "Victoria - Personal (Agatha alerts): Reminder you set: call the pharmacy."

## Frequency and costs
Up to 30 messages per day, usually far fewer. Message and data rates may apply.

## Help and opting out
- Reply **HELP** for help.
- Reply **STOP** at any time to stop all messages. Reply **START** to opt back in.

See the [Privacy Policy](/agatha-sms/privacy) and [Terms](/agatha-sms/terms).
Mobile opt-in data is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing.
